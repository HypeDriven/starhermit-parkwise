    How to play

    Goal

    Slide vehicles along the direction they face until the red car can drive straight out through the glowing exit gate on the right edge of the lot.

    Controls

    • Pointer/touch: tap a vehicle to select, then drag along its axis, or tap a glowing target marker.
    • Keyboard: Tab into the board, then Tab cycles vehicles and arrow keys slide the selected one. Shift+Tab leaves the board for the buttons.
    • U undo, H hint, R restart, C reset camera, Esc pause.
    • Gamepad: D-pad/left stick navigate, A confirm, B cancel, Start pause.

    Scoring

    Score combines a completion base, a bonus for beating the move par, a time bonus, and penalties for hints and blocked attempts. Fewer moves and faster solves score higher.
